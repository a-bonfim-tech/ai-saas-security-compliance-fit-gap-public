# EU AI Act Notes

## Relevance for AI-Enabled B2B SaaS

The EU AI Act is relevant when a product develops, deploys, integrates or provides AI systems in the European market.

For this repository, the EU AI Act is treated as an AI governance and risk classification reference.

## Practical Focus Areas

- AI system description
- Intended purpose
- Risk classification
- Provider, deployer or importer role
- Data governance
- Transparency obligations
- Human oversight
- Logging and monitoring
- Technical documentation
- Risk management
- Post-market monitoring

## Evidence Examples

- AI system description
- Model/provider documentation
- Data flow diagram
- Risk classification memo
- Human oversight procedure
- Logging policy
- User transparency notice
- Evaluation records
- Incident or malfunction reporting process

## Practical Questions

- What does the AI system do?
- Who uses it?
- What decisions or outputs does it influence?
- Does it process personal data?
- Does it affect rights, access, employment, education, finance or safety?
- Is there human oversight?
- Are outputs monitored?
- Are risks documented and mitigated?
