# GDPR Notes

## Relevance for AI-Enabled B2B SaaS

GDPR is relevant whenever the SaaS product processes personal data related to individuals in the European Union or European Economic Area.

For AI-enabled products, GDPR analysis must consider whether personal data is used in prompts, training, inference, logs, analytics, support workflows or third-party AI providers.

## Practical Focus Areas

- Personal data inventory
- Controller and processor roles
- Lawful basis
- Transparency
- Data minimization
- Purpose limitation
- Retention
- Security of processing
- Data subject rights
- Breach notification
- Data processing agreements
- International transfers

## Evidence Examples

- Data inventory
- Record of processing activities
- Privacy notice
- Data processing agreement
- Retention policy
- Security measures
- Access control evidence
- Breach response procedure
- Vendor processing documentation

## Practical Questions

- What personal data is processed?
- Why is the data processed?
- Where is the data stored?
- Who can access it?
- Is it sent to AI providers?
- How long is it retained?
- Can users exercise their rights?
- Is there a breach notification process?
