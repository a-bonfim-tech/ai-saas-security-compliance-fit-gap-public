# ISO/IEC 27001 Notes

## Relevance for AI-Enabled B2B SaaS

ISO/IEC 27001 is relevant because it defines requirements for an Information Security Management System. For B2B SaaS companies, it helps demonstrate that information security is governed, risk-based, documented and continuously improved.

## Practical Focus Areas

- Scope of the information security management system
- Leadership and accountability
- Risk assessment and risk treatment
- Security policies
- Asset management
- Access control
- Supplier relationships
- Secure development
- Incident management
- Business continuity
- Continuous improvement

## Evidence Examples

- ISMS scope
- Security policy
- Risk assessment
- Risk treatment plan
- Statement of applicability
- Access control policy
- Supplier register
- Incident response procedure
- Internal audit records

## Practical Interpretation

For this repository, ISO 27001 is used to translate organizational security requirements into controls and expected evidence.
