## During Initial Discovery

Use the repository as a thinking model, not as a claim of compliance.

Focus on discovering:

- Product scope.
- Architecture.
- Repositories.
- Cloud provider.
- Data processed.
- AI providers.
- Customer security requirements.
- Existing policies.
- Existing evidence.
- Missing evidence.
- High-risk gaps.

## What to Avoid

- Do not claim formal compliance.
- Do not store organization secrets in this repository.
- Do not add real customer data.
- Do not add confidential internal findings.
- Do not publish organization-specific evidence without permission.
- Do not treat AI output as authoritative without verification.

## What to Do

- Keep findings evidence-based.
- Separate assumptions from facts.
- Document sources.
- Ask precise questions.
- Prioritize risk.
- Communicate limitations clearly.
- Convert findings into remediation actions.

I built a practical security and compliance automation lab for AI-enabled B2B SaaS environments, focusing on control mapping, evidence collection, fit-gap analysis, risk prioritization and remediation reporting.
