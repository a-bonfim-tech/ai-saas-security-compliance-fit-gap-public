# Glossary

## AI Governance

The set of policies, controls, processes and accountability mechanisms used to ensure that AI systems are developed, deployed and monitored responsibly, securely and lawfully.

## B2B SaaS

Business-to-business software as a service. A software product delivered through the cloud to business customers.

## Compliance

The ability to demonstrate that an organization follows applicable laws, regulations, standards, contractual requirements or internal policies.

## Control

A technical, procedural or organizational measure implemented to reduce risk or satisfy a requirement.

## Evidence

A verifiable artifact showing that a control exists or operates. Examples include configuration files, logs, policies, screenshots, audit trails, access reviews, CI/CD workflows and documentation.

## Fit-Gap Analysis

A structured comparison between expected requirements and the current state. A fit means the requirement is covered. A gap means the requirement is missing, incomplete or not evidenced.

## Framework

A structured set of requirements, controls, practices or principles used to manage security, privacy, risk or compliance.

## GDPR

The General Data Protection Regulation. It governs the processing of personal data in the European Union and European Economic Area.

## ISO/IEC 27001

An international standard for information security management systems.

## NIST CSF 2.0

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0. It provides a structured approach to governing, identifying, protecting, detecting, responding to and recovering from cybersecurity risks.

## Remediation

An action taken to fix a gap, reduce risk or improve a control.

## Risk

The potential for loss, harm or negative impact resulting from a threat exploiting a vulnerability.

## SOC 2

A reporting framework for service organizations based on Trust Services Criteria such as Security, Availability, Confidentiality, Processing Integrity and Privacy.

## Technical Evidence

Evidence obtained from systems, repositories, configurations, logs, code, workflows or cloud resources.
