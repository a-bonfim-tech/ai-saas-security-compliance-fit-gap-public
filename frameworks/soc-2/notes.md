# SOC 2 Notes

## Relevance for AI-Enabled B2B SaaS

SOC 2 is highly relevant for SaaS companies selling to business customers. It helps demonstrate that the service organization has controls related to trust, security and operational reliability.

## Trust Services Criteria

| Category | Practical Meaning |
|---|---|
| Security | Protection against unauthorized access and misuse. |
| Availability | System availability for operation and use. |
| Confidentiality | Protection of confidential information. |
| Processing Integrity | Complete, valid, accurate, timely and authorized processing. |
| Privacy | Collection, use, retention and disclosure of personal information. |

## Practical Control Areas

- Access control
- Change management
- System operations
- Risk mitigation
- Logical access
- Monitoring
- Incident response
- Vendor management
- Data protection
