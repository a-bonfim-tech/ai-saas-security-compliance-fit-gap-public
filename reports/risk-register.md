# Risk Register

| Risk ID | Domain | Risk Description | Impact | Likelihood | Rating | Recommended Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RISK-001 | Governance | Security responsibilities are not clearly defined. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Define security ownership and document responsibilities. |
| RISK-002 | IAM | Privileged access is not periodically reviewed. | High | Medium | High | Implement access review process and least privilege validation. |
| RISK-003 | SDLC | Code ownership is not formally defined. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Add CODEOWNERS and branch protection rules. |
| RISK-004 | Vulnerability Management | Dependency review is not fully implemented. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Enable dependency review and monitor vulnerable packages. |
| RISK-005 | Logging | Application and cloud logging are not evidenced. | High | Medium | High | Define logging requirements and collect logging evidence. |
| RISK-006 | Privacy | Personal data inventory is missing. | High | Medium | High | Create a personal data inventory and retention model. |
| RISK-007 | AI Governance | AI system purpose, data flow and oversight are not documented. | High | Medium | High | Create AI system documentation and risk assessment. |
